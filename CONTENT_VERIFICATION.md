## 2. Myth vs. History Editorial Standards

1. **King Ravana / Ramayana Traditions**: Classified as `Tradition & Legend`. Never asserted as archaeological certainty.
2. **Sigiriya**: Classified as `Documented History` attributed to King Kassapa I (477–495 CE); popular folklore connecting it to Ravana is explicitly marked as `Local Accounts Vary / Uncertain`.
3. **Seetha Amman Kovil**: Located in Seetha Eliya near **Nuwara Eliya** on Day 5 (25 Dec), NOT on the southern coast.
4. **Ravana Falls**: Located on the Ella–Wellawaya road descent on Day 6 (26 Dec).
5. **Rumassala**: Located in Unawatuna/Galle on Day 6 (26 Dec).
