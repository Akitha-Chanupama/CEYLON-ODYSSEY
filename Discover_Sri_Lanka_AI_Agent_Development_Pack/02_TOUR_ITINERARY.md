## Day 2 — Tuesday 22 December: Sigiriya and Dambulla

### Main stops
1. **Sigiriya Rock Fortress** — UNESCO-inscribed ancient city; fifth-century royal capital associated with King Kassapa I. Key sights include the massive rock, landscape gardens, rock art, and ruins. An optional strenuous climb: do not presume everyone wishes to climb.
2. **Dambulla Cave Temple** — UNESCO-inscribed living Buddhist cave shrine with painted sanctuaries and numerous Buddha images. Respect dress and photo rules.

### Timing logic
- If already sleeping in Sigiriya: schedule the **Sigiriya visit in the cooler morning**, then Dambulla later subject to opening and travel conditions.
- If starting from the airport area: budget a substantial intercity drive; only one major sight may be comfortable. Offer alternatives such as exterior/garden experiences if guests do not want climbs.
- Do not overpromise exact visit times or site hours.

### Ravana narrative integrity
Some tour folklore links Sigiriya to Ravana's palace; **do not state that as accepted archaeology**. Explain Sigiriya's documented King Kassapa history separately from later traditional associations, if discussed.

## Day 3 — Wednesday 23 December: Matale → Kandy

### Proposed experience
- Travel south via **Matale**, stopping at **Sri Muthumari Amman Kovil** when visitor access and rituals allow; it provides a useful living Hindu cultural context.
- Arrive **Kandy**; visit **Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic** only if guests are interested, appropriately dressed, and visiting hours permit.
- Easy walk around **Kandy Lake** and an early finish if needed.

### Host notes
- Explain how Hindu and Buddhist religious traditions coexist in Sri Lanka, and why shrines are active places of worship, not just tourist objects.
- For the Matale kovil, photograph and access rules are site-specific; check with temple staff before entering restricted spaces or photographing worshippers.
- Optional spice garden only if genuinely informative; do not route to high-pressure retail stops.

## Day 4 — Thursday 24 December: Kandy → Ramboda → Nuwara Eliya

### Main stops
1. Scenic highland drive through tea-growing landscapes.
2. **Sri Bhakta Hanuman Temple, Ramboda** — important to the journey's Ramayana theme; within tradition, connected with Hanuman's search for Sita.
3. **Ramboda Falls view** if access/weather/parking allow.
4. Tea-country experience, with an ethical, visitor-accessible factory or tea garden where permitted.
5. Arrive **Nuwara Eliya**, settle in.

### Flexible alternative
If the group arrives early, **Seetha Amman Kovil** could move to late 24 December, making 25 December less rushed. Only do this if worship timings and daylight allow.

### Practical notes
- Nuwara Eliya is cooler than the coast: suggest an extra layer and rain protection.
- Mountain roads can be slower than map distance suggests; avoid back-to-back time-bound activities.

## Day 5 — Friday 25 December: Seetha Amman Kovil → Ella

**Narrative priority: Very high.** This is the strongest full day to build around Sita/Ravana storytelling.

### Priority sequence
1. **Seetha Amman Kovil, Seetha Eliya**, near Nuwara Eliya. Official provincial tourism describes it as around **5 km from Nuwara Eliya town** and roughly **1 km from Hakgala Gardens**.
2. **Hakgala Botanical Gardens** (optional) — often connected in Ramayana-oriented local tradition with Ashoka Vatika; clearly label this as a traditional association.
3. **Divurumpola** (optional stop on the broader Welimada/Bandarawela direction) — associated in tradition with Sita's ordeal by fire. Respect living religious practices.
4. **Ella** — arrival and accommodation; relaxed viewpoints or a short walk depending on daylight.
5. **Ravana Cave** *only if time, daylight, weather, access and guest mobility permit*. The path involves steep/uneven steps; never guarantee this alongside all optional stops.

### If the day becomes overloaded
Keep **Seetha Amman Kovil** and an unhurried arrival in **Ella**. Choose **one** optional site, not all. Ravana Falls is conveniently positioned for the southbound drive on 26 December.

### High-value storytelling arc
"The temple, the gardens, the hills and the Ella caves are part of a landscape that many people associate with episodes of the Ramayana. We can appreciate these beliefs while separating them from archaeology."

## Day 6 — Saturday 26 December: Ella → Ravana Falls → Unawatuna / Rumassala

### Primary sequence
1. Depart Ella early enough to avoid a rushed evening.
2. **Ravana Falls** on the **Ella–Wellawaya road** — roadside viewpoints; **do not climb wet rocks, cross barriers or enter waterfall pools**.
3. Substantial road journey toward **Unawatuna / Galle area** (planning allowance roughly **4.5–6.5 hours**, potentially longer with weather, rest stops, Christmas traffic or route changes; not a live ETA).
4. **Rumassala (Rumasala)** near Unawatuna — featured in Sri Lanka Tourism's Ramayana itinerary as a place associated with the Sanjeevani-mountain tradition; choose a suitable public and accessible viewpoint/path.
5. Relax in **Unawatuna** and enjoy the coast.

### Optional: Galle Fort
If arrival is early enough and guests want a historical walking experience, visit **Galle Fort**. Do not promise Rumassala + long transfer + long Galle visit in a single day.

### Safety
- Swim only where locally supervised and conditions are safe. Current flags, lifeguard guidance and currents matter more than general seasonal averages.
- If hills experience heavy rain/landslides, check actual road status and plan a safer route with the driver.
