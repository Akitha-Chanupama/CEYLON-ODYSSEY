## 4. The myth / archaeology distinction

- UNESCO's Sigiriya entry offers strong evidence for Kassapa-era history. It **does not** confirm a Ravana palace.
- Provincial tourism and Sri Lanka Tourism publish Ramayana-related site descriptions as local religious/tourism traditions; important for culturally sensitive interpretation, **not** proof of literal epic geography.
- Temple ritual and legends should be presented respectfully and clearly attributed.
- If sources disagree about the epic interpretation, avoid pretending one version is universally accepted. Use "In one popular tradition…" or "Many local accounts associate…".

## 6. Weather and season

Sri Lanka Tourism describes **December–February as the northeast monsoon** season, while the south/west coast generally enters its favorable travel season. However, the island has highly varied terrain and weather. Never issue location-specific forecasts or promise safe beaches from this general seasonal information. Link to the Department of Meteorology and consider rain-friendly alternatives.

## 7. Visa guidance caveat

Visa/ETA policies have undergone updates and differ by nationality and entry category. **Do not state that all visitors are visa-free or that ETA is universally required/waived.** Link only to official Immigration and ETA portals; prompt the host/guests to verify their specific circumstances. There is no reason to store passport or visa numbers in the site.

## 10. Source rechecking workflow

Before launch and daily during the tour:
1. List important stops for the next 48 hours.
2. Recheck the official provider for hours/prices/access and local weather/roads.
3. Update relevant fields with verification date.
4. If still uncertain, display `Ask your host` / `Confirm before visit`.
5. Have a fallback option for the day; publish actual confirmed changes.

Never use an AI-generated answer alone as verification of a time-sensitive operational fact.
